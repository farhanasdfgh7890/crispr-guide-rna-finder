How it works

1
Validate
Cleans your input, strips FASTA headers, converts to uppercase, rejects invalid characters.
2
Scan PAMs
Searches both strands for NGG motifs (SpCas9 PAM). Reports position and strand for each hit.
3
Extract guides
Pulls the 20 nucleotides immediately upstream of each PAM. Removes duplicate sequences.
4
Score & rank
Calculates GC%, applies an 80-point scoring system, and predicts off-target risk using weighted Hamming distance.

Scoring system

RulePointsReason
GC content 40–70%+30Optimal editing efficiency
GC content 30–40% or 70–80%+15Acceptable range
No poly-T run (≥4)+15Avoids U6 promoter termination
No poly-A run (≥5)+10Avoids transcript instability
No repeated 4-mer+10Reduces off-target binding
Last nucleotide is G+10Improves cleavage efficiency
First nucleotide is G+5U6 transcription preference
